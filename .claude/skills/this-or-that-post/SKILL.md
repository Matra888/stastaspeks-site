# "Šis vai tas" post: thought process

Work through the steps in order. Each step has one sentence. Do not skip step 1.

## Step 1: Unusual claim (slide 1)
- Say one thing most people in the field would not say out loud.
- It must be clear what the topic is (marketing, sales, pricing, funnel).
- Format: "Ne visi X vēlas Y." or "Bildini klientu jau pirmajā dienā."
- Test: could a reader repeat it to a friend in one breath?

## Step 2: Comparison A (slide 2)
- Pick a category where the number is fast or high.
- Headline: "Dažas X dara Y uzreiz" (plain claim).
- Pill: the metric definition in 3-5 words (e.g. "Konversija pēc nozares").
- 3-4 cards: category and range (e.g. "Pārtika un dzērieni: 3,8–6,2 %").
- Use only numbers the user provides, with the source noted outside the post.

## Step 3: Comparison B (slide 3)
- Same structure, opposite side (slow category).
- Headline: "Citām vajag ilgāku laiku" or equivalent.
- Keep the same pill text as slide 2 so the two read as one comparison.
- Use the accent colour that differs from slide 2 if the user wants contrast.

## Step 4: Question and invite (slide 4)
- One relatable question about the reader's own business.
- Add one sentence that links back to the claim in slide 1.
- Invite a comment and a save in one line, with the bookmark icon.
- Format: "Kā ir tavā nozarē? Vai izdodas saņemt „Jā“ uzreiz, vai tomēr jāpacenšas ilgāk?"

## Output format for each post
1. Slide texts (4 slides), Latvian, with macrons.
2. Caption: one hook line, 2-3 short lines explaining the difference, one save/comment line, 3-6 hashtags.
3. Render format: `ig_carousel` (1080x1350) and `linkedin` (1200x1200).

## Quality checks
- Each slide has one job.
- Numbers match the user's source table.
- No invented claims or statistics.
- The question on slide 4 can be answered by the reader in one word.

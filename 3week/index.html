Questions

How much does it cost?

Price and payment details are shared when you sign up. We recommend Claude Pro (€18/month). Ask for a 7-day Pro trial code at stastaspeks@gmail.com.

Can I do it on the basic plan?

Mostly. Most tasks work on the basic plan, which includes projects and artifacts, but usage limits are lower, so you may need to pause. Saving your design system (Task 2) and the video edits bonus need a paid plan.

I've only used Claude a few times. Is that okay?

Yes. Task 1 covers the basics, and every video shows the result first, then each step on screen.

It's also a skill worth having. In a 2026 Windsor.ai study of 35,000+ marketing teams, teams analysing data with Claude outnumbered those using OpenAI tools about 7 to 1.

How much time do I need?

Eight tasks plus a bonus, delivered Monday, Wednesday and Friday over three weeks. Plan about 30 minutes per task, including a video of 10 minutes or less. That's under two hours a week.

What's the format?

Short recorded videos you can watch whenever suits you. Questions and sharing happen in the WhatsApp group.

Are there live sessions?

No. Everything is recorded, and there's no fixed schedule. A lesson every second day keeps the pace steady without pressure to cram.

Questions

How much does the challenge cost?

The challenge itself is free. To get the most out of it, we recommend Claude Pro (€18/month). Want to try Pro first? Email stastaspeks@gmail.com to ask about a 7-day free Pro trial code.

Can I do it with the free version of Claude?

Partly. Most tasks work on the free plan, which includes projects and artifacts, but with lower usage limits, so you may need to pause and come back later. Saving your own Claude Design system (Task 2) and the video edits gift need a paid plan.

I’ve only used Claude a few times. Is that okay?

Yes. The first task covers the basic skills and project setup, and every video shows each step on screen, so beginners can follow along easily.

It’s also a skill worth having. Claude is quickly becoming the go-to AI for marketing data: in a 2026 Windsor.ai study of 35,000+ marketing teams, teams analyzing their data with Claude outnumbered those using OpenAI tools about 7 to 1, and Claude handled around 81% of AI-driven data queries on their platform.

How long is the program, and how much time do I need?

3 weeks. There are 8 tasks, and they arrive by email, one at a time. Plan about 30 minutes per task, including a video of 10 minutes or less. That’s under 2 hours a week.

What’s the format?

Short recorded videos. Each one shows you the result first, then walks you through how to do it step by step. Questions and sharing happen in the WhatsApp group.

Are there live sessions?

No, everything is recorded, so you can watch whenever it suits you. There’s no fixed schedule, so you can take each task at your own pace.

Can I join later than the start?

Yes. Join any time. Tasks are sent by email, starting with the next Monday, 23 November, so you won’t miss anything.
